Fracture City

They told Newsman Ned it was temporary. The broadcast ended anyway, and he has been in the block world since. Mine it, build in it, drive through it, talk to the people who live there — and press shift+Z if you would rather it were a siege. There is an arcade inside the city that runs the other games on this page.

What a beta means here Your progress is saved in this browser only — clearing site data clears it, and it does not follow you to another machine. There is no sound yet. It needs a keyboard and a mouse, so phones and tablets are out. On a modest laptop the city can be heavy; the other games are light.

Present Palm

Made as a hub of small self-contained things rather than one large thing. Each app on this page is a single HTML file that runs on its own, keeps its own data on your machine, and talks to the others only when you ask it to.

Found something broken? That is the point of a beta — say what you were doing when it happened.
