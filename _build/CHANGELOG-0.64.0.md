# Fracture City 0.64.0 · Sodium noir, voices, the wire

## Added
- Sodium noir grade (sodium-noir.js). Every day has its own off-colour
  daylight: Bile Green, Bruise Violet, Rust Day, Chemical Teal, Nicotine Noon,
  Ash Rose or Oxide Haze. Online, everyone sees the same hue on the same UTC
  date; offline it changes at each in-game midnight. Nights are sodium:
  orange light from above on streets and roofs, teal on walls, an orange
  haze on the horizon, and brighter than before.
- Light pools and wet sheen under every point light near the player, as
  painted decals (no extra real lights), on every quality tier.
- Display panel (F): SODIUM GRADE toggle, showing today's hue.
- NPC conversations (npc-voices.js). Talking to a resident opens a
  conversation with up to six replies (click, tap or 1 to 6): introduce
  yourself, hear their story in three parts across visits, district rumours,
  their take on Ned, goodbye, or brush them off. About 140 new lines across
  the ten roles and shopkeepers.
- NPC memory, per account in this browser: whether you have met, your name if
  you gave it, how often you have talked, how far into their story you got,
  what you last discussed, and whether you were rude. Greetings change with it.
- Real news in conversation, online only. Couriers, broadcasters, clerks,
  vendors and shopkeepers sometimes quote a real headline from the Common
  Ground wire: verbatim, tagged REAL NEWS with source, with "Where's that
  from?" giving the date and link. Headlines about death, violence, war,
  disasters, crime, illness or politics are filtered out; nothing older than
  three days is used.
- Two lighter news sources: The Conversation (Atom, CC BY-ND 4.0) and The
  Public Domain Review. The news parser now reads Atom as well as RSS.

## Not changed
- Original barks are kept and still open a first meeting. Story characters
  and cast members keep their own lines.
