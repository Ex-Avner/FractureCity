# Fracture City 0.86.0 · Seamless bodies

## Joints
- Arms and legs are now single closed limbs that carry the colour of
  whatever covers them. Before, sleeves, shorts, socks, tattoos, fishnets
  and rips were separate shells laid over the skin at nearly the same
  size, so the two flickered against each other at hems and joints, and
  tattoo bands floated off the arm like bracelets.
- Hems are hard edges with a small fabric lip: a tee sleeve ends cleanly
  on the upper arm, shorts end above the knee, socks sit on the shin.
- Elbows and knees end in rounded caps that overlap, so a bent arm or
  leg stays one smooth piece instead of opening a gap or showing the
  inside of the tube. Shoulders and hips have ball joints in the colour
  of the sleeve or trousers.
- Tube winding fixed: limb surfaces face outward, so lighting is right
  and back-facing details no longer show through from the far side.
- The shirt hem now sits just outside the trousers at the waist instead
  of flickering against them.

## Clothes
- Kilts and long coat tails hang from their own bone and swing with the
  stride, so a step forward no longer pushes the thigh through the
  fabric.
- Mesh tops are painted onto the torso as a net instead of bars floating
  in front of it.
- Tattoos (bands, blackwork, circuit, sleeve), fishnets, ripped knees and
  plaid lines are painted on the limb.
- Gloves now cover the fingers too.

## Cost
- A figure is about 15 % more triangles on average for the crowd (pattern
  detail is only sampled finely where it needs it). The worst case stays
  under the wardrobe test's 16,000-triangle budget.
