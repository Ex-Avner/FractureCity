# Fracture City 0.80.0 · The punk wardrobe

## A new figure
- Every resident, rival and player is rebuilt as a stylised toon figure:
  one toon material with a four-step light ramp, neon parts that glow.
- Painted faces: eyes that blink, brows, a mouth that moves while a
  resident talks to you, makeup, piercings and tattoos.
- Real joints: elbows, knees and fingers bend; the torso sways at rest.
- Lighter than the old figure: 5k to 13k triangles per person (was about
  18k).

## The wardrobe
- Tools · "Wardrobe · your look" (or the old avatar panel) opens it.
- Live preview: drag to turn, Face / Full body, Randomise.
- Six presets: Gutter Punk, Goth, Riot Grrrl, Skater, Raver, Office Drone.
- Groups:
  - Face: eye shape, brows, makeup (liner, smoky, tears, bolt, glam,
    skull), face width and length, jaw, nose.
  - Hair: buzz, crop, mohawk, liberty spikes, fauxhawk, spiked, undercut,
    mullet, long, pigtails, bun, dreads, bald, with dyed tips; headgear
    from a beanie or bandana to a helmet, mask or crown.
  - Top: shirts (band tee, mesh, tank…) and jackets (leather, studded,
    denim vest, bomber, hoodie, plaid, long coat, tailored).
  - Legs: skinny, ripped, plaid, cargo, kilt, shorts; fishnets or socks;
    combat boots, creepers, high-tops, steel toes, platforms.
  - Extras: shoulder spikes, studded belt, wallet chain, collar, fingerless
    gloves, patches, pins, shades, neon trim; piercings; tattoos (bands, circuit, blackwork,
    sleeve, neck, face).
  - Body: frame (slim, average, broad, curvy), height, shoulders.
  - Colours: palettes for each colour slot, or any custom colour.
- Esc cancels; "Wear it" saves.

## Rare pieces
- The base wardrobe is free. Eleven rare pieces cost credits: skull and
  glam makeup, liberty spikes, dreads, the crown, the studded jacket, the
  kilt, platform boots, shoulder spikes and neon trim.
- Try anything on; "Wear it" stays locked until you buy what you are
  trying on. Each piece is bought once.
- Online, rare pieces are bought from the server wallet and the server
  checks ownership: a look with unowned pieces falls back to defaults.
- Solo cities keep their own unlocks; Creative unlocks everything.

## The crowd
- Residents dress by scene: punks, goths, skaters, ravers, workwear and
  office. Forty people give about forty different looks.

## Online
- Your look is sent when you join and whenever you save the wardrobe.
- Other residents appear as they dressed.

## Known limits
- The arena multiplayer mode still uses the old figure.
- Credits for rare pieces come from the same capped wallet as 0.79.
