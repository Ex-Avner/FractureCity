# v0.71.0 — Furniture and objects

- The room workshop now opens a visual furniture catalogue: 40 grouped designs across seating, tables, storage, bedroom, lighting, kitchen, bathroom, studio and décor.
- Search, category browsing, rendered 3D thumbnails and four coordinated colour stories replace the supply-list entry point. Structural building and advanced art remain available.
- Choose an object to return to first-person placement with a complete translucent preview. Place, turn, undo, browse and finish controls work on touch screens. T rotates the whole design.
- Furniture surfaces support smaller decorative objects, including lamps on desks. Groups retain the existing art editing, local saves, online ownership checks and piece limits.
- Furniture is decorative; it does not add functional appliances or player collision. Structural building remains the way to make solid walls and floors.
- Includes the unreleased v0.70.0 Atelier frontage, Ledger input and responsive mobile fixes.

Validation: furniture catalogue/placement/persistence/mobile tests, city-art, art-collection and city-build browser suites. Prior v0.70.0 checks are recorded in its changelog.
