Present Palm

The hub. An ecosystem of interconnected HTML5 applications around the Triadic Coherence framework and the Fracture City world. Each app lives in apps/<slug>/ with a fracture.app.json manifest declaring its name, inputs, and outputs.

Apps run in sandboxed BrowserWindows opened by Present Palm. Inside the hub, they receive a window.fracture API: save, read, openApp, emit, subscribe, getProvider. Outside the hub, the same HTML still works — it falls back to localStorage and per-app provider config.

Based on P.R. Erickson's Triaxial Orientation / Coherence framework.
