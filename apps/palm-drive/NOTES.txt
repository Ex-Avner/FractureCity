PALM DRIVE — build notes
════════════════════════

RENDERER
  Pseudo-3D, not real 3D. The road is z-ordered segments projected with a
  perspective divide and drawn back-to-front as trapezoids (Pole Position /
  OutRun technique). Locked 60fps, and it gives the "road bends into the
  horizon" feel real 3D tends to flatten.

ADDING A TRACK
  Add a row to TRACKS: {id, name, sky, laps, sprites, desc, build()}.
  build() is a sequence of addStraight / addCurve / addHill / addSCurves.
  Skies are data in SKIES — stops, sun, ridges, city, ground, fog, grass.
  buildTrack() appends addDownhillToEnd so the loop rejoins at y=0.

CALIBRATION NOTES (learned the hard way — do not "simplify" these)
  • RIVAL SKILL MUST STRADDLE THE PLAYER'S RANGE, NOT SIT ABOVE OR BELOW IT.
    A competent human sustains roughly 0.62 (scruffy) to 0.90 (near-ideal) of
    top speed over a race. Two failures have now been made here:
      - 0.85-1.00: whole field above the player. Beaten by half a lap, always.
      - 0.575-0.70: whole field below the player. Unlosable.
    Both cliff-edge, because a NARROW band sits entirely on one side of the
    player. The six are now spread 0.66-0.86 so each finishing position means
    something, and the overall level is set by the difficulty tier instead.
  • THE 0.575-0.70 CUT WAS A WORKAROUND FOR THE GRID BUG, NOT A BALANCE FIX.
    Rivals used to spawn at trackLength-N, which read as a lap ahead (see
    below). Skill was cut to compensate. The grid bug was then fixed and the
    skill cut was never reverted, which is how the game became unlosable.
    If you find a "balance" constant, check what bug it was hiding first.
  • GRID POSITIONS MUST BE POSITIVE OFFSETS FROM THE START LINE.
    Rivals were spawned at trackLength-N. Since progress = (lap-1)*trackLength
    + z, that read as almost a full lap AHEAD, so the player was classified
    last no matter how fast they drove. This looked exactly like a balance
    problem and was not one.
  • CATCH-UP ONLY EVER HELPS A RIVAL ADRIFT OF THE FIELD, AND ONLY ONE THAT IS
    BEHIND YOU. A band that pulls back cars you have already beaten reads as
    cheating and removes the point of driving well.
  • FRACTURE TIER IS 1.045, NOT HIGHER. That puts the quickest rival at ~0.90
    — level with a near-ideal human lap. At 1.09 the tier was unwinnable even
    for a perfect drive, which is not difficulty, it is a wall.
  • STEERING IS SPEED-SENSITIVE (steerAuth = 1 - speedPct*0.38).
    Lateral movement already scales with speed via dx, so without this the car
    is knife-edge at the top end — measured 24s spent off-road on the FASTEST
    circuit purely from oversteer. Dropped to 1s with the fix.
  • Car sprite world width must stay well under a lane (road spans 2*ROAD_W
    across 3 lanes, so a lane is ~1466). At 1500 traffic read as a moving wall.
  • Track hills must roughly cancel. An early layout climbed 48,000 units and
    dropped it all at the end, which projected as a cliff.

RIVAL EXPANSION (rivals.js)
  Loads after the inline script and overrides spawnRivals / updateRivals.
  Three tiers (ROOKIE 0.88 / PRO 1.00 / FRACTURE 1.045) multiply a base skill
  band, and each of the six has a TEMPERAMENT that changes behaviour when the
  player is near: blocker (covers the line), drafter (gains in a tow), closer
  (faster on the final lap), opportunist (lifts when you go off), metronome
  (no pace drift), wildcard (fast, occasionally throws it away). Per-circuit
  bias means the running order differs by track.
  Balance is asserted by _sim.js — run it after touching any constant.

NEWSMAN NED (ned.js)
  Announcer + campaign. Ned is canon: see apps/works/data/book.json ch.21,
  "THE MONK WHO DID NOT BROADCAST". He addresses "citizens", reports only what
  can be confirmed, and says so on air when the facts run out. He does NOT
  hype — the comedy is in the epistemics. Keep new lines in that register.
  The campaign derives a NEMESIS from who actually finishes ahead of you, so
  the season narration responds to real results rather than a fixed script.

HOST BINDING GOTCHA
  The inline script declares its state with const/let (CAREER, RIVALS, G,
  rivals, segments...). Those are global LEXICAL bindings and are NOT
  properties of globalThis, so an add-on script cannot reach them as
  globalThis.CAREER. rivals.js captures them into globalThis.PDHost — use
  that. Function declarations (startRace, updateRivals...) ARE on globalThis
  and can be overridden directly.

PERSISTENCE
  localStorage 'palmdrive_career_v1' — per-track lap records, race/win/podium
  counts, trophies, and the in-progress championship (round, points, results)
  so a season survives a reload and can be resumed from the menu.

VERIFIED
  All four circuits complete with an autopilot; lap times 40-61s; championship
  awards points and produces standings; career survives reload.
  _sim.js     — balance grid across 3 tiers x 4 driving standards. Asserts
                losing is possible at PRO/FRACTURE, winning is possible at
                every tier, and better driving always improves position.
                Current: PRO gives P6.8 (bad) / P5.0 (average) / P3.0 (good)
                / P1.0 (expert). FRACTURE: P7.0 / P6.0 / P4.3 / P1.5.
  _sim_dom.js — loads the game in jsdom, drives the tier UI, runs 600 frames
                of rival AI, and exercises the campaign + nemesis logic.
