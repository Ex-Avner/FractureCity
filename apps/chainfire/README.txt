CHAINFIRE (working title)

Play: open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge), desktop or phone.
Everything is in that one file. The Fracture City edition uses system fonts and makes no external font requests.

Controls: drag anywhere, pull back, release.

Saved data (progress, shards, shop purchases, best score) lives in the browser's local storage, per device.

Contents of version 4: 8 hand-built levels plus endless generated levels, a recurring boss,
star ratings, a shop (12 balls, 5 arenas, 4 blast styles), and an adaptive swarm that learns
your habits and unlocks one new counter-tactic per level.

Fracture City integration: play at the Common Ground east-side cabinet, or open Chainfire from Present Palm. Leave cabinet returns to the city. Progress is local to this browser/device.
