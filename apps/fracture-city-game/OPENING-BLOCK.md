# Civic Core streetscape, v0.43.0

The first intersection now has a coordinated art-deco treatment across FCN, the Bureau, the Residue Room and Echo Heights: dark ceramic frames, brass reveals, thin neon channels, glazed display bays, lit entrance canopies, street signs and flush paving details. FCN's screen and mast receive a matching frame and crown.

Removed the three obsolete social echoes, their floating cubes, glowing ground squares and interaction hotspots. Current tutorial witnesses, keeper, Wardens, repair crews and quest speakers remain. Ambient pedestrians are hidden near the intersection during the prologue.

Sidewalks extend two metres toward the intersection. Metre-wide generated paint strips become narrow lane markings and crosswalks. The Bureau's two eleven-metre neon pylons and projecting colonnade are removed; a stepped entrance preserves access to its doorway.

Structural changes happen during generation before saved voxel edits are applied. Existing player edits are not erased. Decorative facade pieces are grouped into instanced batches, use no additional point lights or shadow casters, and are installed once per chunk. They add visual detail to the existing voxel buildings; they are not replacement high-resolution building models.

Verification: four authored chunks, bounded detail batches, no legacy echo hotspots or avatars, removed pylons, clear carriageway, traversable kerb, retained doors, no repeated decoration, no browser exceptions. Full first-night browser test verifies every tutorial beat and saved class/weapon/heat state.
