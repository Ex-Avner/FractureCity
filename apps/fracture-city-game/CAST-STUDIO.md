# Cast Studio — 0.55

Open **Radio & menu → Create characters**, or press **F6** after entering a city.

Create a character with a rotating 3D preview. Customize hair, skin, clothing colors, outfit, tattoos, piercings, build, height, face width, jaw, nose and shoulders. Write a name, personal story, ambition, greeting and response about the city. Select a weapon, save the design, and choose Spawn nearby. Rogues have an explicit Spawn hostile rogue button and appear farther from the player.

- Residents wander near their placement and speak only when approached. An armed resident stays peaceful.
- Allies follow or hold a position. Armed allies defend the player from nearby hostiles.
- Rogues attack players and allies, using visible attack wind-ups. Peaceful mode suppresses combat.

Approach a resident or ally and press E or tap the talk button. Dialogue uses the text you authored; no account or external AI service is needed. Allies can be commanded from conversation or the roster. Downed characters disappear; Revive nearby restores them. Dismiss removes the placement, keeping the design.

The device stores up to 32 designs and 12 placed characters per city. Cast data autosaves and is flushed by the game's save action. Placements are keyed to the saved city's seed; designs remain available across cities. This is local single-player cast creation, not shared multiplayer publishing. Export characters to move designs between devices; import validates the file and creates new IDs. Export does not include city placements.

Custom weapons use balanced NPC attacks; the player-only arc-chain and megaphone effects are not duplicated by custom NPCs. Characters use shared collision, grounding and bounded route planning. Unloaded distant actors are hidden, and follow commands never teleport them through the city.
