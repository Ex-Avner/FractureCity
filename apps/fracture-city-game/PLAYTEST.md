# First-time player session — 30 minutes

Use a new save in 0.37.1; keep the tester’s existing saves. Let someone who has not seen development play without coaching. Record the build, machine, display resolution and quality setting. Obtain their permission before recording their screen or voice.

1. Ask them to start a game and say what they think is happening. Record time to first movement and first understood objective.
2. Let them finish the prologue. Record unclear choices, repeated instructions, missed interactions and deaths.
3. Observe the first repair: can they find the breakers, hold E, recognize the defense radius and understand why progress pauses?
4. Ask them to switch view, select a weapon, dodge an attack and ride the skateboard. Record clipping, awkward poses and moments when the item or target is hard to see.
5. Save, return to the menu and continue. Ask what they expect to be preserved, then compare it with the resumed state.
6. Ask: Who is Ned? What caused tonight’s problem? What are you trying to restore? What would you do next? What was satisfying, confusing or slow?

Success targets: tester identifies the next action without coaching, can explain the repair pause, recognizes the equipped weapon, and resumes the expected progress. Do not count an automated accelerated playthrough as evidence of pacing or newcomer comprehension.

Log each issue with timestamp, expected behavior, observed behavior and reproduction steps. Prioritize lost progress, blocked objectives and control confusion before cosmetic preferences. No external newcomer session has been completed as part of this release.
