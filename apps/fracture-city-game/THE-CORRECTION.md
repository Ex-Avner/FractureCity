# The Correction, v0.42.0

Ned opens a Bureau test file during his bulletin at 17:58. It spreads through the city network, turns residents, and leaves public systems enforcing absurd instructions. The player helps five residents restore those systems and recover the original order. Ned finally broadcasts the evidence and stops the test.

The five chapters follow one causal chain: Rook restores the clock; Inez isolates the clean carrier; Leon powers the archive; Daniel authorizes the correction using the recovered order; the player reroutes the transmitter feeds. Each final wave contains a named civic boss, counted inside the existing twenty-enemy objective. Existing completed chapters remain completed. An unfinished boss respawns at full health after loading, like other unfinished wave enemies.

Bosses: the Meter Maid (targeted fine zone), Sponsored Forecast (larger, slower weather zone), Debt Collector (ring with a safe center), Acting Deputy Deputy (cross-shaped stamp), Official Denial (faster, wider ring). Colored ground warnings precede damage. After each special attack there is a 2.5-second window of increased damage received. Their ordinary attacks retain existing wind-up behavior. This pass uses the existing character models with civic sign props; it is not a new sculpted character asset set.

Ambient dialogue is recorded without opening a caption. Chapter callbacks resolve without waiting for reading; one short instruction appears for seven active-play seconds. The journal includes the last sixty messages for this session. It resets when creating/loading a city; chapter briefings remain readable from the journal. Decisions and the opening's authored scenes remain explicit player interactions.

Compact HUD has one current campaign objective. The repeated objective list, outbreak count, news ticker, and redundant repair prompt are suppressed. The repair hold bar, health, weapons, interaction cues and boss warnings remain visible. Full HUD remains available in Radio & menu.

Four local optional quests join the existing six: The Pedestrian Subscription, Rain Now With Ads, Soup Pending Approval, and His Worship the Bin. They use the existing quest choices, save data and credit rewards. These extra quests are for the solo city, not newly assigned multiplayer maps.

Netlify build includes the desktop and touch controls, existing arcade, and thirteen soundtrack files. Physical iOS testing and a public deployment are separate from this release.
