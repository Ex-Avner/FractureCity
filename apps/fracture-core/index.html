FRACTURECORE

Your three books, plus whatever model is running on this Mac. Four tabs, and they do quite different things.

Ask

A conversation. Before it answers, it searches the books and hands the model whatever is actually relevant, so an answer about your work is built on your words and cites which passage it used. Ask it anything else too, and it just answers.

Read

The search on its own, no model needed. Describe a passage and it finds it. Switch to phrase mode for every single place an exact wording appears across all three books.

Train

Making a model that writes in your voice. It cannot run that itself, so it picks the right one for this Mac, writes the exact commands, and tests the result against the untrained model at the end.

Engine

Which model answers. This is the tab that decides how smart everything else feels.

The one thing worth knowing: this app has no intelligence of its own. The search inside it knows where everything in your books is and cannot write a sentence. Every written answer comes from a model running outside it. If the answers seem thick, the Engine tab is where that is fixed, not here.
