## 0.64.0 · Look and voices

- `apps/fracture-city-game/sodium-noir.js` (`window.Grit`): a different sky
  hue every day (7 hues: Bile Green, Bruise Violet, Rust Day, Chemical Teal,
  Nicotine Noon, Ash Rose, Oxide Haze). Online, the UTC date picks the hue;
  offline, in-game midnights advance it. Nights are sodium orange from above,
  teal on walls, and bright. Painted light pools and wet sheen under point
  lights. Display panel (F) has a SODIUM GRADE toggle.
- `apps/fracture-city-game/npc-voices.js` (`window.NPCVoices`): talking to a
  resident opens a conversation, up to six replies (click/tap/1–6). About 140
  lines across ten roles plus shopkeepers. Memory per account in
  localStorage `fc-npc-memory:<username>`: met count, name, story progress,
  rudeness.
- Real news in NPC lines, online only: some roles quote a headline from
  `/api/news` verbatim, labeled REAL NEWS with source and link. The `HEAVY`
  regex filters death, violence, war, disasters, crime, illness, politics;
  nothing older than 3 days.
- `server/news-feed.cjs` reads Atom as well as RSS; two new sources: The
  Conversation and The Public Domain Review. **Unverified:** check that
  `/api/news` on Render lists both with `available: true`.

## 0.65.0 · The Seam (this push)

New files: `apps/fracture-city-game/the-seam.js`, `city-wildlife.js`,
`THE-SEAM.md`; test `_test/the-seam.test.cjs`. Script tags added after
`npc-voices.js` in `index.html`.

- **District:** chunks 10..11 × 0..1 (world x 240–288, z 0–48), about 70 m
  east of the Main Beta arrival. Overrides `Chunk.prototype.generate` for
  those keys (outermost wrapper). Road ring kept; interior heaved one block
  (keeps cars out); a fissure with a glowing core; 12 raised beds; the
  Ledger shed with its counter facing the road at (244.2, 24.5). Floating
  street slabs, sparks, Retraction-fence tape. Wayfinding sign at Common
  Ground (177.5, 30.5).
- **Blocks:** ids 150–153 (`seam light`, `heaved ground`, `seam soil`,
  `broken street`). Block ids are a save format; do not reuse.
- **Layout constants** `BEDS`, `WARDS`, `FISSURE` at the top of
  `the-seam.js` are a save format (plot and ward indices). Do not reorder.
- **Loop:** plant (credits or free packets) → real-time growth → harvest →
  sell at the Ledger Market → clear plots, build/upgrade 6 lamp-wards, raise
  the fence. Ranks by harvest count gate plots, ward count and level.
  Crops: wheat 4 min, melon 12, corn 30, tuber 90 (needs a ward).
- **Market:** each crop matches a topic regex over `/api/news` titles; more
  matches → higher price; each sale today lowers it 3%. The top matching
  headline is shown quoted, labeled REAL NEWS, linked.
- **Misprints:** enemy type `misprint` added to `ENEMY_TYPES`/`HITBOX`,
  spawned as normal `Enemy` instances with an instance `update`
  (`misprintUpdate`), so player weapons hit them. Night only, player inside
  the Seam, not in peaceful mode. They seek the nearest planted bed, eat
  (sets the crop back 35%), flee to the crack; fight back if shot. Wards
  zap in range. Kills give credits (normal `die()`) plus ink.
- **Away raids:** `simulateAway()` every 1.5 h away, max 6, defence = wards
  + fence + guard pets vs. strength; report in the Ledger.
- **Almanac:** keyed off `Grit.hue.id`: Wheat Rush, Static Bloom, Echo
  Season, Tuber Night (double growth for one crop and that creature
  migrates), Misprint Moon (bigger raids, double ink), Rust Market (+25%
  prices), Quiet Haze (no raids).
- **Creatures:** moth, pigeon, eel, cat. Feed the favourite crop 3× (2× on
  migration day) to tame; max 4. Modes guard / follow / rest.
- **City wildlife** (`city-wildlife.js`, `window.FCWildlife`): moths around
  lit street lamps at night, pigeon flocks that scatter, eels arcing out of
  the road at night. Owns the species meshes the Seam reuses.
- **Persistence:** localStorage `fc-seam:<username>` (per account, this
  browser only). Uses the device clock.
- **Test API:** `window.FCSeam` (`skew(ms)` fast-forwards time, `plant`,
  `harvest`, `sell`, `spawnMisprint`, `spawnWild`, `feed`, `simulateAway`,
  `open(tab)`, `reset()`).
